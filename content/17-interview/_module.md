---
title: Interview problem sets
summary: About 45 original interview-style problems in three tiers, covering the patterns interviewers ask about. Ties, NULLs, top-N, streaks, retention, funnels, cohorts, sessions and hierarchies.
---
These problems combine everything in the course, the way interview questions do. Each one names its dataset. Open *Database tables* to see the columns. The difficulty tags are a guide: *easy* needs one or two ideas, *hard* needs several working together.

Treat each one like a real interview. Read carefully, decide on the output's grain (one row per what?), write it in steps with CTEs, check the edge cases (ties, NULLs, duplicates, empty groups), and only then submit. Efficiency is graded too.
