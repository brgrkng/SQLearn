---
title: Security and administration essentials
summary: Roles and privileges, row-level security, SQL injection, VACUUM and statistics, finding problems with pg_stat views, and an overview of the operations side.
---
The last technical module covers the SQL that keeps a database **safe** and **healthy**: who may see and change what, how to keep untrusted input from becoming code, and the maintenance work PostgreSQL does in the background.

Operations topics with little SQL to practise (backups, replication, high availability, configuration) get a short overview with pointers for further study.
