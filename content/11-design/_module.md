---
title: Database design and normalization
summary: From requirements to tables. Entities and relationships, keys, the normal forms, deliberate denormalization, and proven modelling patterns.
---
A good schema makes correct queries easy and wrong data impossible. A poor one forces every query to work around it, forever. This module is about designing tables: turning a description of the world into entities, relationships and keys, recognising redundancy with the **normal forms**, and knowing when to break the rules on purpose.

The design exercises give you the table and column names, so the checks can test your work, and leave the decisions to you: keys, relationships, constraints, and which column goes in which table. Like everything else, they're graded by behaviour.
