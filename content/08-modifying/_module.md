---
title: Modifying data
summary: INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE; RETURNING; upserts with ON CONFLICT; MERGE; and data-modifying CTEs that move rows in a single statement.
---
Reading data is most of SQL, but sooner or later you change it. This module covers the statements that write: inserting rows, updating them, deleting them, and "insert or update" in its various forms.

Every exercise runs on a fresh copy of the database, so experiment freely. Exercises are graded by **checking the state of the tables afterwards**: the same checks run against your database and the reference solution's, and their results must match.
